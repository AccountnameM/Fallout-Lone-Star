# New Rule: Third Fatigue
There is a new fatigue level, called Debilitated. It applies a -3 to all checks and Pace. This is to balance the brutal Radiation effects, as well as Hunger, Thirst, and Bumps and Bruises.

# Radiation Rules Change
To "gamify Radiation, instead of being a Disease, it deals special RAD damage. Each player will have a Radiation Pool of 4 RAD points; when the pool is filled, you take a point of Fatigue, which doesn’t go away until cured via RadAway. Environmental Radiation is notated with X rad/time (such as 2 rad/minute, 4 rad/hour, or 1 rad/round). Radiation attacks simply deal that many RADS. When you succeed or raise on the check, you reduce RAD damage by one (Ex; You have been in a 3 rad/10min area, and make a VIG check at the end of 10 minutes. On a fail , you take 3 RADS. Success brings it to 2, raise to 1, and two raises to 0).


# Starting Funds
The new Starting Funds total is &dollar;200. Spending 1 hindrance point only grants $100, instead of &dollar;400, for the purpose of a balanced economy. This will let you get ammo, food, and non-combat gear, but not necessarily better equipment without heavy hindrance point investment.See [[Fallout Gear]] document for prices.

# Survival and Travel
An average human needs to eat 1 pound of food and drink 2 pounds of water a day. Follow the Thirst and Hunger rules in the core book. Foraging yields 1lb of food for a successful Survival check, whereas Hunting yeilds meat based on the quarry (see [[Fallout Gear|PLAYER GEAR]] page 2).
When traveling, if you are not within a marked location or planned event, the DM will check for random encounters. Random encounters always involve a check for Treasure, Rads, Weather, Occupation, and Wildlife, as well as a suitable location created, likely on the fly.
Travel Speeds have their own bonuses and penalties;
1. Difficult Terrain (Rocky, uneven)= .5x Speed
2. Literal Fucking Mountains = .25x Speed
3. March; 2 watches max, full speed
4. Forced March; Each watch past of a march requires a VIG-2 vs Fatigue roll.
5. Hustle; 2x speed for an hour, VIG vs Fatigue with cumulative -2 for each hour thereafter. -4 to Notice, Stealth, and Navigation checks.
6. Cautious; .75x speed, +4 Notice, Stealth, and Navigation
7. Exploration; .5x speed, double Encounter checks, Survival checks may be made to find food
8. Camping; halted rest, ability to engage with complex tasks that require a stationary workspace. Any amount of time may be spent camping, as long as such time is tracked.

Characters move a number of miles an hour equal to half their pace. A 24 hour day is split into six 4 hour Watches; 2 travel Watches, two resting Watches, and two sleeping Watches. Actions taken are assumed to be the sum total of accomplishment during this time frame.

Every day PCs spend scrapping, they gain &dollar;100 multiplied by the location scrap value.
