# Background Edges


### Arcane Background (Psycher)

- Skill; Psyonics (Smarts)
- Power Points; No PP
- Powers; 2 + [[Detect-Conceal Arcana|Detect/Conceal Arcana]]
- **Power Selection**; [[arcane protection]], [[blind]], [[beast friend]], [[Boost-Lower Trait|boost/lower trait]], [[confusion]], [[deflection]], [[Detect-Conceal Arcana|detect/conceal arcana]], [[dispel]], [[divination]], [[empathy]], [[entangle]], [[fear]], [[havoc]], [[illusion]], [[invisibility]], [[mind link]], [[mind reading]], [[mind wipe]], [[object reading]], [[puppet]], [[slumber]], [[Sound-Silence|sound/silence]], [[stun]], [[telekinesis]]

**Special**; Psychic Powers are typically difficult to control. If the user moves or attempts Multi-Actions, they incur a further -2 penalty to concentrate on Psychic Powers. If they hold still and only focus on bringing out their power, ignore this penalty.

**Backlash**; Dynamic Backlash table has the following change; Short Circuit applies -1 to all checks for 1d3 rounds.
**Psycher recommendations**; to further depict your Psychic powers, take the [[Danger Sense]] or [[Alertness]] edges.

**REQUIREMENTS**: Novice

### Lead Belly (Background)
**REQUIREMENTS**: Novice, Vigor d6+

Ignore VIG checks for Radiation from consuming dirty food or water.


### Rad Child (Background)
**Requirements:** Novice, Vigor d6+

When you fill your Radiation Pool, you heal 1 wound. Ghouls have their Radiation Pool reduced to 2.


### Iron Bones (Background)
**Requirements:** Novice, Vigor d8+

When you are Wounded, roll a d6. On a 5-6, you are not Injured.

### Old One (Background, Racial)
**Requirements:** Novice, Ghoul or Robot

Some ghouls have been around for a very long time, learning lessons that stick with them. Similarly, some robots have been absorbing and integrating new data for a very long time.

An Old One has a wealth of knowledge and experience, granting a +2 to all Common Knowledge rolls and +2 to one of: Academics, Battle, or Science.

### Super Mutant Master (Background,Racial)
**Requirements:** Novice, Super Mutant, Smarts d8+

Your mutant is either a First Generation super mutant, or has developed a fully functioning brain. Regardless, this mutant no longer suffers the -2 penalty to Smarts rolls.

### Thick Skin (Racial)
**Requirements:** Novice, Super Mutant, Spirit d6+, Vigor d8+

A mutant's hide can grow over time, thickening into a ridiculously strong leathery layer. This skin provides +2 physical armor.


# Combat Edges

### Pain Train (Combat)
**Requirements:** Seasoned, STR d8, Athletics d6

If you take the run action, you ignore the Run Penalty to Athletics Tests or Grapples you initiate, and gain a further +2 to the check, so long as you move at least ½ your pace before you reach your target.

### Crusher (Combat)
**Requirements:** Veteran, STR d8, Fighting d6

Ignore 2 points of Called Shot penalties and gain AP+2 for Fighting.


### Moving Target (Combat)
**Requirements:** Veteran, AGL d8

If you take the Run action, ranged attacks against you take a -2, and you get a +2 to AGL saves until the start of your next turn. You don’t need to cover that much distance; simply declare your action, and any “missing” pace will be assumed to be evasive movement.

### Fast Hands (Combat)
**Requirements:** Novice, AGL d6, Shooting d8

If your action card is a Face card, Reloading is a free action.


### Gun-Fu (Combat)
**Requirements:** Shooting d6, Fighting d6

Gain +1 Parry from holding at least 1 gun (does not stack with 2 guns). Two Handed guns may be used in Melee. One- Handed guns in melee get +1 to attack.

### Mysterious Stranger/Miss Fortune (Combat)
**Requirements:** [[Luck]]

When you spend a benny to reroll an attack, roll a d20. On a 20, MS/MF appears and applies 1 wound to your target.

### Nerd Rage! (Combat)
**Requirements:** Heroic, SMT d10

When you are wounded 2 for the first time in a scene, your STR and VIG become equal to your SMT die until the end of the scene. This ability recharges when you get back to full health.

### Analytical Fighter (Combat)
**Requirements:** Veteran, SMT d8, Notice/Psyonics d8

You may calculate your Parry using Notice or Psyonics. You may use Notice or Psyonics in place of Fighting when edges require it.


### Shotgun Surgeon (Combat)
**Requirements:** Novice, Shooting d8

Shotguns gain AP2.

### Shotgun Blaster (Combat)
**Requirements:** Seasoned, Shooting d8, STR d6

When you hit an enemy with a Shotgun blast at short range, they must make a STR check (-2 on a raise) or be knocked Prone.


### Overcharge (Combat)
**Requirements:** Seasoned, Science d8, Shooting d6

You may Overcharge energy weapons, consuming double ammo and adding an additional die of damage. If you roll a 1 on any of your Shooting dice, after you land your hits and roll damage, the energy weapon overheats and must recharge for 1 full round before it can be used again.

### Disintegrate (combat)
**Requirements:** Heroic, Science d10, Overcharge

When you kill an enemy with an Overcharged shot, they explode, dealing 2d6 damage to each entity adjacent to them.

### Explosive Soul (combat)
**Requirements:** Veteran, Repair d6, Athletics d6

+2 to set, hide, and detect Traps, auto-cook Grenades
you throw, increase Launcher ranges +5/+10/+20.

### Pyromania (combat)
**Requirements:** Novice

When you check to catch an enemy on fire, increase the ignition chance by 1. Checks to put out fires you start take a - 1. Deal +1 damage with Fire.

### Power Armor Training (combat)
**Requirements:** Soldier, Connection to a viable faction

Able to wear, repair, and modify Power Armor.

# Psycher Edges

### Combat Psycher (Psycher)
**Requirements:** Seasoned, Smarts d6, Psionics d8

When you take this edge, pick two from between blast, bolt, burst or smite. You gain those powers. 

### Psychically Sensitive (Psycher)
**Requirements:** Novice, Spirit d6, Psionics d8 

+2 to check people's emotions. This applies to mind reading and object reading, and stacks with empathy. 


### Precognition (Psycher)
**Requirements:** Psychically Sensitive, [[Danger Sense]], Smarts d8

You occasionally see visions of the future. They happen quickly, are almost always vague and hard to decipher, but they usually warn of some dangerous event that might save your life—and your friends as well. Remind the DM of this edge at the beginning of the session. At some point in the session, when it seems most appropriate, the DM will describe a likely upcoming event or scene. The future is mutable, so it may not come to pass, but it should be a likely course, or perhaps the vision depicts a path not to take and won’t come to pass unless your psycher ignores the warning. 

Make a Spirit roll immediately after seeing the vision — the DM may modify this depending on the severity of the vision. If the roll is failed, your character is Fatigued for the session. This might manifest as violent nosebleeds, migraines, or simple weariness.
