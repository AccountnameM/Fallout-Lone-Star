A mutation of humans, ghouls look like walking corpses, despite being startlingly healthy. Ghouls are made, not born - ghouls are sterile, unable to reproduce. New ghouls are formed by extended radiation exposure.

- **Ugly:** Ghouls are horrid, deformed humanoids damaged by radiation. They must take [[Ugly (Minor & Major)|Ugly (Major)]] hindrance.

- **Atrophy**: They also experience chronic pain, taking a -1 to Vigor rolls (but not Toughness) and, if they have 1 or more wounds, take a further -1 to their total wound penalties.

- **Rad Life:** whenever they fill their Radiation Pool, they *heal* 1 wound. They ignore all other Rad effects.They are also ignored by Feral Ghouls unless provoked.

- **Ghoulification/Feralization:** each time any player gains Fatigue from Radiation, the DM and Player may decide that person begins the Ghoulification - or if a ghoul already, the Feralization - Process.
