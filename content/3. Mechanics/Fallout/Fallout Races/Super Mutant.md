A powerful, artificial mutation of humanity, super mutants are stronger, tougher, larger, and more resilient than humans. This is for a Second Generation super mutant from the west coast (the ones created by accident), or any super mutant from the east coast.

- **Big:** About 9 feet tall and around 400 pounds, super mutants are massive beings that need specialized gear. Subtract 2 from Trait rolls when using equipment that wasn't designed for super mutants or power armor. Most equipment, clothing, and food costs double.
- **Dumb:** Second generation mutants are not smart. They tend to have limited vocabularies and very simple sentence structures. Their problem solving is usually limited to "hit it, harder". They have a -2 to all Smarts rolls.
- **Immune to Disease:** An aggressive immune system, and unnatural genetic structure, makes super mutants immune to any disease in the wasteland and effectively ageless.
- **Impulsive:** Powerful and stupid, super mutants are naturally Impulsive, prone to acting without considering the consequences. 
- **Radiation Resistance:** +4 to resist radiation and reduce radiation-based damage -4.
- **Size:** Size 2, which increases Toughness by 2.
- **Strong:** Super mutants are incredibly strong, starting with a d8 Strength. This increases their maximum Strength to d12+2.
