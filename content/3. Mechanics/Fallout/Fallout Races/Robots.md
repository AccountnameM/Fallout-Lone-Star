While most robots are governed by the distorted remains of their pre-War programming, some robots achieve true sentience and are appropriate as playable characters. There are a few features universal to robots, followed by descriptions of the models most likely to become properly aware.

**Constructs:** Robots  add +2 to, recover from being Shaken, ignore one level of Wound modifiers, don’t breathe, and are, immune to disease and poison. Wounds must be mended via the Repair skill. Each attempt, takes one hour per current Wound level and ignores the “Golden Hour.”

**Made for work:** They gain a Skill Specialty, adding +2 to any chosen skill. They gain +2 Armor, but -1 Toughness.

**Can't Swim**: All robots have the [[Can’t Swim (Minor)]] Hindrance

**We don't serve your kind:** Bots are [[Outsider (Minor & Major)|Outsiders (Major)]], considered property, and have no legal rights in much of the wasteland.

**Powered:** They must consume 1 Energy Cell charge every hour, or its Equivalent as decided by the DM. The bot may go offline to conserve energy, but it must be manually rebooted to turn back on.

Bots may acquire Mods, as described in the Robots section of the [[Fallout Gear#ROBOT CRAP|FALLOUT GEAR]] document. Bots are considered BIG due to their inhuman shape, and must spend double on Armor and Weapons that aren’t in the MOD section.
