**Rank**:Novice
**Power Points**:1
**Range**:Smarts
**Duration**:Until the end of the victim’s next turn
**Trappings**:Hypnotic lights, brief illusions, loud noises.

Confusion confounds a target, making him both Distracted and Vulnerable if he fails a Smarts roll (at −2 with a raise on the arcane skill roll). Both states are removed at the end of the victim’s next turn.


# Modifiers
- **AREA EFFECT (+2/+3)**: For +2 points the power affects everyone in a Medium Blast Template. For +3 points the area of effect is increased to a Large Blast Template.
