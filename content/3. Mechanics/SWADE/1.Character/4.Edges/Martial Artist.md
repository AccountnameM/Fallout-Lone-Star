**REQUIREMENTS**: Novice, Fighting d6+

The fighter has trained in basic martial arts. Her fists and feet are weapons (see [[Rules#Natural Weapons|Natural Weapons]], page 104) so she’s always considered armed. She adds +1 when striking with them and causes Strength+d4 damage. If she already has a Strength damage die from the [[Ancestries#Positive Ancestral Abilities|Claws]] ancestral ability (page 18) or the [[Brawler]] Edge, increase the damage a die type. 

Martial Artist does not add to damage from other Natural Weapons such as fangs or horns.
