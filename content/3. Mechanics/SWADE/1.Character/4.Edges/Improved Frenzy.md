**REQUIREMENTS**: Veteran, [[Frenzy]]

As [[Frenzy]] but the fighter adds a third Fighting die to his Fighting attack made with Frenzy this turn

>Example: Red is desperately battling a giant crab-like creature on an alien planet. She has Improved Frenzy and decides to attack three times (a Multi-Action at the usual penalty). She rolls her Fighting skill with two extra dice for her first attack. She doesn’t get the extra dice on her second or third Fighting rolls.
