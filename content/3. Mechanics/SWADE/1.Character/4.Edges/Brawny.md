**REQUIREMENTS**: Novice, Strength d6+, Vigor d6+

Your bruiser is very large or very fit. Her Size increases by +1 (and therefore Toughness by 1) and she treats her Strength as one die type higher when determining [[Gear#Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] (page 67) and Minimum Strength to use armor, weapons, and equipment [[Gear#Minimum Strength|without a penalty]] (page 66).
Brawny can’t increase a character’s Size above +3.
