This adventurer is very skinny, very short, or both. His [[Rules#Size & Scale|Size]] (see page 106) is reduced by 1, which reduces his Toughness as well.
Size can’t be reduced below −1, but the Toughness penalty remains. A Small half-folk, for example, remains Size −1 and loses a point of Toughness.
