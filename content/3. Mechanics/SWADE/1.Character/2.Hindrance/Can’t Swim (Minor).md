Most people in the 21st century can swim thanks to swimming pools, easy travel to lakes and beaches, or educational efforts. Historically, how- ever, those who weren’t raised around a temperate body of water could not. 
Characters with this Hindrance suffer a −2 penalty to the Athletics skill when swimming and each inch moved in water costs 3″ of Pace. See Movement (page 92) for more details and [[The AdventureTool kit#Drowning|Drowning]] (page 126).
