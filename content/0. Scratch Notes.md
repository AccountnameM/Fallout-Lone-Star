- A shipment of Twinkies on the side of the road is a trap by a small group of young, inexperienced, and stupid raiders. They will miss their first shot.

- A wild Yao Guai attacks the party 
- Giant Horned Lizards
- A pack of feral ghouls emerges from nearby and attacks the party.

HEARTS—STRANGERS: 
- A group of settlers offer the party food and water, and share information about nearby locations.
- A friendly wandering trader offers to trade supplies or information with the party.
- The party comes across a traveling medic who offers to heal any injuries or ailments.
- A lone wanderer asks for directions and offers to share some useful knowledge in return.
- A charismatic wastelander invites the party to a makeshift campfire for a friendly conversation.
- A local farmer offers the party fresh produce in exchange for help dealing with pests on their farm.
- A Missionary preaching the word of god
